Oxtore

Multi-store retail & reseller platform · Freelance · 2025 – now

Going to production

A retail platform where stores sell directly and supply resellers on consignment, with stock, staff and sales tracked per store.

  • Designed the platform as Spring Boot microservices behind Spring Cloud Gateway, with Eureka service discovery and Kafka events.
  • Stock service: per-store inventory with real-time decrement on sale, wholesale versus retail stock, and partial or full reclaim.
  • Reseller service: consignment selling, inter-store supply requests with approval, and role-based price visibility.
  • Staff service: per-store roles (supervisor, manager, salesman) enforced with AOP authorization, plus real-time WebSocket notifications.
  • Secured with JWT, containerized with Docker, and shipped through CI/CD with quality gates.

DAD

Real-time blood product management · GPI France · part of the SentinelEyes programme

Pilot at 3 sites

Manages blood product inventory, reservation, delivery and quarantine. It drives connected refrigerated cabinets and RFID readers, and runs on FHIR messaging in line with EFS standards. Rollout is planned for every transfusion site in France.

  • Designed an end-to-end audit trail with hash chaining and RabbitMQ event streaming, giving tamper-proof traceability.
  • Delivered a real-time notification system (SSE, Redis, Observer pattern), from backend service to Angular dashboard.
  • Built the stateful blood-bag delivery workflow with real-time fridge orchestration: lock and unlock signals, RFID LED blink commands and WebSocket-driven inventory checks, with automatic rollback when the wrong bag is picked.
  • Implemented a dual RFID and barcode integrity check at handover, with automatic quarantine on mismatch to prevent misplaced-chip incidents.
  • Generated the legally required French transport document at the end of each delivery, for full compliance with EFS regulations.
  • Technical lead on the frontend and backend refactoring (TDD, DDD): task allocation, mentoring and delivery follow-up.
  • Built a GPT-4 pipeline that extracts lab-report fields into validated JSON.
  • Set up per-microservice CI/CD (GitLab CI, SonarQube quality gates) and Ansible playbooks to configure client-site machines.
  • Contributed to requirements analysis and wrote technical documentation with UML.
